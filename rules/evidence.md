# Evidence Rules

Public credibility depends on traceable evidence.

## Proof before claims

- Material claims must be proportional to direct evidence.
- Keep claim-to-source relationships inspectable and close enough for review.
- Prefer omission or narrower wording when evidence is incomplete.
- Repository presence, source code, tests, or commit activity alone do not prove deployment or operation.

## Evidence states

Use these distinctions when they improve accuracy:

- `IMPLEMENTED`: the capability exists in source/artifacts.
- `VERIFIED`: defined checks directly passed.
- `ACCEPTED`: an authorized review or acceptance decision exists.
- `DEPLOYED`: deployment to a stated environment is directly proven.
- `OPERATED`: real operation/use is directly proven.

Never collapse these states into a decorative score or imply a later state from an earlier one.

## Claim integrity

Do not invent or inflate:

- employment or employer relationships;
- freelancing or client work;
- customers or users;
- revenue or commercial outcomes;
- adoption;
- seniority or years of experience;
- performance gains;
- certifications;
- deployment;
- production operation;
- business outcomes or metrics.

Separate personal responsibility from repository-wide, team, platform, or third-party outcomes.

## Media evidence

- Generated or decorative media is not factual engineering evidence.
- Generated media must not masquerade as screenshots, customer evidence, metrics, deployment proof, operational proof, or outcome proof.
- Factual screenshots or recordings must be attributable to the represented system/state when used as evidence.
