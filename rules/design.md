# Design Rules

These rules define durable presentation constraints for the public portfolio.

## Communication

- Optimize for recruiter scanning before decorative novelty.
- Use concise plain English.
- Maintain strong visual hierarchy, descriptive headings, and layer-cake readability.
- Keep evidence and responsibility easier to find than implementation trivia.
- Avoid a generic AI-template appearance.

## Prohibited presentation patterns

Do not use:

- skill/proficiency bars;
- technology icon walls as primary communication;
- fake terminals;
- fake dashboards;
- fake metrics;
- invented testimonials;
- spectacle-driven interfaces that obscure evidence.

## Motion and media

- Motion must be restrained and optional.
- Static content must remain complete and understandable without animation or video.
- Media authoring format and provider are implementation details, not product semantics.
- Do not introduce Three.js, WebGL, 3D assets/pipelines, or spectacle-driven 3D presentation unless future approved architecture explicitly restores that requirement.

## Accessibility and resilience

- Use semantic structure.
- Preserve keyboard operation and visible focus behavior.
- Maintain sufficient contrast and responsive layouts.
- Support reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not place unique required meaning only in motion, hover state, imagery, or video.
